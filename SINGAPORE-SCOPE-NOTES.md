# Singapore HDB scope decisions for drafting

These notes adapt the supplied outline to the chosen reader: a homeowner new to DIY in an HDB flat. They are editorial decisions for the manuscript, not a complete compliance guide. The original chapter outlines are preserved in `outline/` so they can be compared with the draft. The author chose to keep uncommon HDB examples as brief exceptions.

| Outline item | Drafting decision | Basis to check in the project profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6.2 Outlets, switches, dimmers, light fixtures | Make this a **hire a Licensed Electrical Worker** profile for installation work. The homeowner can define the brief, check credentials, and document the result. Keep plug-in devices and bulb changes in a separate green scope. | [EMA guidance](https://www.ema.gov.sg/consumer-information/electricity/engaging-licensed-workers); [HDB electrical guidelines](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/renovation-guidelines/electrical-works). |
| 5.5 and 6.1 Taps, showerheads, toilet fittings | Split by the exact task. PUB lists some simple plumbing works separately from regulated work. Do not group tap replacement, toilet-bowl replacement, and pipe alteration into one DIY permission claim. | [PUB plumbing works](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Professionals/Requirements/Licensed-Plumbers/Plumbing-Works); [HDB plumbing guidelines](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/renovation-guidelines/water-and-sanitary-plumbing-works-and-gas-works). |
| 5.3 Mounting and shelves | Define a limited, light-duty job. Check HDB's conditions for the wall type and fixing, concealed services, and the load. Heavy or uncertain loads leave the green scope. | [HDB building works](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/renovation-guidelines/building-works). |
| 5.2 Tub recaulk and 7.2 Shower waterproofing | Keep cosmetic seal replacement separate from floor or wall waterproofing. A leak into a lower flat, failed membrane, or work on bathroom finishes needs the HDB conditions and a professional scope. | [HDB building works](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/renovation-guidelines/building-works); [HDB renovation information](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/important-information-on-renovations). |
| 5.11 Gutters and 6.10 deck/fence staining | Keep as brief transfer examples outside the primary HDB scope. | Author preference; no regulatory claim. |
| 6.6 Exterior sealing | Keep the outline entry but explain homeowner observation and professional execution in an HDB flat. | [HDB window guidance](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/home-maintenance/home-care-guide/windows). |
| 6.5 Smart thermostat and 6.1 garbage disposal | Keep as brief exceptions, with locally useful doorbell and tap guidance taking the profile space. | Author preference; installation rules still apply. |

## Rule to carry into every chapter

A project's color describes a **bounded homeowner task**, not permission to perform regulated work. The HDB permit question, the licensed-worker question, and the practical risk question are separate checks. HDB notes that some electrical works need no HDB permit yet remain subject to its guidelines; EMA separately directs consumers to Licensed Electrical Workers for electrical installation work. [HDB electrical guidelines](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/renovation-guidelines/electrical-works), [EMA guidance](https://www.ema.gov.sg/consumer-information/electricity/engaging-licensed-workers).
