# 1. Executive Summary
- Thesis: DIY should be guided by **risk management**, not by “Can I learn this?”
- Green-light work offers high reward, low risk, and cheap failure.
- Yellow-light work is possible for motivated homeowners but requires training, permits, and pro backup.
- Red-light work is not worth learning for DIY execution because the downside is catastrophic, hidden, illegal, or uninsurable.
- Core rule: **Own the brief. Hire the risk.**
