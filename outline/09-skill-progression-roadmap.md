# 9. Skill Progression Roadmap
- **Level 0:** Observe, learn tools, shutoffs, safety.
- **Level 1:** Green core — painting, caulking, mounting, hardware.
- **Level 2:** Green advanced + Yellow prep — drywall, simple plumbing.
- **Level 3:** Yellow with training — electrical replacements, faucets, tiling.
- **Level 4:** Pro-assisted or licensed — red work.
- Certifications: first aid, electrical safety, asbestos/lead awareness, trade courses.
