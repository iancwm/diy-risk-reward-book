# 5. Green Light Activities — Learn Boldly
For each activity: reward, risk, tools, time, cost, skill level, common mistakes, when to stop.

## 5.1 Painting and Prep
- Surface prep, patching, sanding, priming, cutting in, cleanup.
- Highest reward, lowest risk.
- Common mistakes: poor prep, wrong primer, overpainting, drips.

## 5.2 Caulking and Sealants
- Silicone vs. acrylic vs. polyurethane.
- Wet-area vs. paintable vs. exterior.
- Backer rod, tooling, cure time, removal of old sealant.
- Common mistakes: wet surface, too-large gap, wrong product.

## 5.3 Hanging and Mounting
- Pictures, mirrors, curtain rods, shelves, TV brackets.
- Studs vs. anchors vs. toggle bolts.
- AC/cable detection, pilot holes, weight ratings, leveling.
- Common mistakes: missing studs, wrong anchor, drilling into wires/pipes.

## 5.4 Hardware Swaps
- Cabinet knobs, handles, hinges, towel bars, switch plates.
- Low risk, high visual reward.

## 5.5 Simple Plumbing Fixture Repairs
- Aerators, showerheads, toilet flappers, fill valves, p-traps, supply lines.
- Learn shutoff, drain, disassembly, washers/O-rings, leak testing.
- Stop if: hidden leak, corroded valve, water won’t shut off.

## 5.6 Drain Clearing
- Plunger, drain snake, p-trap removal.
- Avoid chemical drain cleaners.
- Stop if: sewage backup, multiple drains clogged, chemical exposure.

## 5.7 Smart Home Plug-Ins
- Smart bulbs, plugs, cameras, hubs, network basics.
- Avoid hardwired electrical unless trained.

## 5.8 Furniture Assembly and Non-Structural Shelving
- Flat-pack, closet organizers, storage.
- Avoid load-bearing or ceiling-mounted heavy items.

## 5.9 Appliance Maintenance
- Vacuum filters, clean fridge coils, replace dryer vent hose, gaskets.
- Stop if: gas appliance, sealed refrigerant system, control board.

## 5.10 Weatherstripping and Basic Insulation
- Doors, windows, gaps, drafts.
- Avoid interfering with ventilation or combustion air.

## 5.11 Simple Outdoor Maintenance
- Gutter cleaning, deck staining, fence painting, basic landscaping.
- Yellow if ladders, heights, or exterior envelope sealing.
