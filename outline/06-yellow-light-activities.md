# 6. Yellow Light Activities — Learn Carefully
For each: prerequisites, permit triggers, safety controls, testing, when to call a pro.

## 6.1 Replacing Faucets, Toilets, Garbage Disposals
- Water shutoff, supply lines, drain alignment, leak testing.
- Permit may be required.
- Stop if: soldered copper, corroded shutoff, sewage, no access.

## 6.2 Basic Electrical Replacements
- Outlets, switches, dimmers, light fixtures.
- Breaker off, voltage tester, wire gauge, box fill, grounding.
- Permit often required.
- Stop if: aluminum wiring, knob-and-tube, no ground, panel issues, burning smell.

## 6.3 Drywall Repair
- Patching, taping, mudding, sanding, priming.
- Low risk but high skill curve.

## 6.4 Tiling a Backsplash
- Not a shower or wet room.
- Substrate, adhesive, grout, sealing.
- Stop if: waterproofing needed, large format stone, structural movement.

## 6.5 Smart Thermostat or Doorbell
- Low-voltage wiring, HVAC compatibility, transformer load.
- Stop if: line voltage, no C-wire, HVAC control board.

## 6.6 Exterior Caulking and Sealing
- Envelope joints, windows, doors.
- Stop if: rot, structural gaps, roof penetrations.

## 6.7 Building Simple Furniture or Floating Shelves
- Non-structural, into studs, weight-rated.
- Stop if: heavy loads, cantilever, ceiling mount.

## 6.8 Minor Plumbing Supply Line Replacement
- Braided lines, shutoff valves, compression fittings.
- Stop if: behind wall, soldered, slab, main line.

## 6.9 Appliance Installation
- Dishwasher, microwave, range hood.
- Stop if: gas, 240V, hardwired, ducting changes.

## 6.10 Deck and Fence Staining
- Cleaning, prep, application.
- Stop if: structural rot, height, permits.
