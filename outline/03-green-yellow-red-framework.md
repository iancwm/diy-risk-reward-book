# 3. The Green / Yellow / Red Framework
## 3.1 Green Light — High Reward, Low Risk
- Visible, accessible, reversible, isolated.
- No permit, no life-safety risk, no hidden water or power.
- Failure is cheap: crooked shelf, uneven caulk, scratched paint.
- Examples: painting, caulking, mounting, hardware swaps, simple fixture repairs.

## 3.2 Yellow Light — Moderate Risk, Conditional DIY
- Involves water, electricity, load, exterior envelope, or moderate cost.
- Requires theory, correct tools, local code checks, and sometimes permits.
- Failure can cause damage, minor injury, or costly rework.
- Examples: replacing a faucet, swapping a light fixture, tiling a backsplash, installing a smart thermostat.

## 3.3 Red Light — High Risk, Not Worth DIY Learning
- Life safety, structural, gas, mains electrical, waterproofing, hidden systems.
- Failure can cause fire, flood, explosion, collapse, mold, carbon monoxide, legal liability.
- Requires license, permit, inspection, specialized tools, insurance.
- Learning it for execution is a poor use of time because the risk is asymmetric.

## 3.4 Risk Scoring Model
- **Likelihood** of failure
- **Severity** of consequences
- **Detectability** — will you know before it becomes catastrophic?
- **Reversibility** — can it be undone cheaply?
- **Code/insurance impact**
- **Skill/tool/time cost**
- Decision rule: If any red-flag factor is high, stop and hire.
