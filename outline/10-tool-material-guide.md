# 10. Tool and Material Guide
- **Starter toolkit:** drill/driver, stud finder with AC detection, level, tape measure, voltage tester, adjustable wrench, pliers, caulk gun, PPE.
- **Green tools:** sanding block, putty knife, paint tray, anchor kit.
- **Yellow tools:** multimeter, pipe wrench, drain snake, tile trowel, GFCI tester.
- **Materials:** caulk types, anchors, wire nuts, PEX vs. copper, backer rod, grout, sealant.
- **Rent vs. buy:** specialty tools for one-off jobs.
