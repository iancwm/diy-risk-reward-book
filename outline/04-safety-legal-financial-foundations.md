# 4. General Safety, Legal, and Financial Foundations
## 4.1 Legal and Administrative
- Local building codes and permit triggers
- HOA rules and rental agreements
- Insurance policy exclusions and liability
- Warranty voiding
- Resale disclosure and inspection risk

## 4.2 Safety Basics
- Know shutoffs: water main, fixture valves, breaker panel, gas valve.
- PPE: eye protection, gloves, mask, ear protection.
- Electrical: breaker off, lockout/tagout, voltage tester.
- Water: drain lines, test for 24 hours, watch for slow leaks.
- Fire: extinguisher, no open flame near gas or solvents.

## 4.3 Financial and Time Reality
- DIY is not free: tools, materials, mistakes, time.
- False economy: a $200 DIY fix that causes $20,000 damage.
- Opportunity cost: pros often finish faster and warrant work.
- Documentation: photos, receipts, permits, inspection records.
