# Appendix A: Green / Yellow / Red Master Table

**Use:** Find the exact task, read its bounded scope, then apply the decision form in Appendix B and the HDB/worker check in Appendix C. A colour is a starting classification for a Singapore HDB beginner, not a permit or a guarantee. If the work expands, classify the new scope again. “Professional execution” means the homeowner plans and checks the job while the qualified person performs it.

## Green: bounded homeowner tasks

| Project | Green scope | Transfer out of green when… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interior painting (5.1) | Sound, dry wall and cosmetic preparation | Dampness, loose concrete, spreading/wide crack, recurring mould |
| Caulking (5.2) | Dry, accessible decorative joint | Leak, wet substrate, loose tile, suspected waterproofing failure |
| Hanging/mounting (5.3) | One light item, confirmed wall and fixing | Heavy or overhead load, uncertain services or household-shelter condition |
| Hardware swaps (5.4) | Cabinet or furniture fitting with existing holes | Wall, window, security, or electrical fitting |
| Simple fixture care (5.5) | Visible listed accessory, such as showerhead or cistern mechanism | Pipe or valve alteration, hidden leak, toilet-bowl replacement |
| Drain clearing (5.6) | Visible debris at strainer or floor-trap cover | Sewage, multiple affected drains, serious/recurrent blockage |
| Smart plug-ins (5.7) | Plug-in or battery device with no fixed wiring | New point, hardwired device, damaged or overheating socket |
| Furniture assembly (5.8) | Freestanding unit built to maker's instructions | Wall/ceiling fixing, heavy unstable unit, unsatisfied anti-tip requirement |
| Appliance maintenance (5.9) | User-serviceable filter or surface | Gas, refrigerant, fixed wiring, control board, exterior unit/support |
| Weatherstripping (5.10) | Removable interior-door strip that preserves operation and airflow | Window repair, fire/shelter door, ventilation or security change |
| Outdoor maintenance (5.11) | Brief transfer example only | HDB facade, window exterior, common property, height |

## Yellow: conditions must be written before work

| Project | Homeowner scope in this book | Transfer to professional execution when… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tap replacement (6.1) | Accessible like-for-like listed simple task, sound shutoff, leak checks | Valve/pipe damage, concealed leak, toilet bowl or new drainage |
| Electrical replacements (6.2) | **Brief, hire, and verify** a Licensed Electrical Worker | Physical replacement or wiring work is professional execution under EMA guidance[^ema-electrical] |
| Drywall patch (6.3) | Small dry non-structural board defect, no services | Moisture, movement, concrete, concealed service |
| Kitchen backsplash (6.4) | Small dry-wall finish within HDB conditions | Existing finish removal, hacking, wet area, electrical changes |
| Smart control/doorbell (6.5) | Battery/plug-in product; mounting and privacy check | Fixed wiring, transformer, uncertain air-con controls |
| Exterior sealing (6.6) | **Document and brief** a qualified repair | Facade/window exterior repair is professional execution in this HDB scope |
| Floating shelf (6.7) | Modest load on confirmed wall/fixing | Heavy/overhead load, household-shelter or service uncertainty |
| Flexible hose (6.8) | Listed visible hose, reliable shutoff, repeat leak checks | Stop valve, fixed/hidden pipe, corrosion, unknown adaptor |
| Appliance installation (6.9) | Existing suitable plug/simple connection, maker-compliant fit | Gas, hardwiring, new drain/duct, exterior air-con installation |
| Deck/fence staining (6.10) | Brief transfer example only | Outside typical HDB-flat scope |

PUB's published list is the starting source for distinguishing simple from regulated plumbing; confirm the exact task and current HDB conditions before treating it as a homeowner project.[^pub-plumbing][^hdb-plumbing] HDB's building guidance controls the relevant wall, finish, and household-shelter details.[^hdb-building]

## Red: homeowner briefs, qualified people execute

| Work or warning sign | Why it is red for DIY execution | Homeowner's useful action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wiring, switches, sockets, lighting points, consumer unit | Licensed Electrical Worker boundary; shock/fire and concealed defects[^ema-electrical] | Describe symptoms, verify licence, ask about testing and HDB conditions |
| Piped-gas pipes or appliances | Licensed gas service work and tested handover[^ema-gas] | Verify worker, retain safety statement; use emergency guidance for a suspected leak |
| Structural members, wall hacking, uncertain load path | HDB approval/qualified assessment; failure may affect other occupants[^hdb-building] | Document proposal or crack; ask HDB and Qualified Person where required |
| Bathroom waterproofing or ceiling leak to another flat | Hidden membrane/screed failure and cross-flat damage[^hdb-leaks] | Document, coordinate with neighbour/Town Council, commission diagnosis and repair |
| Regulated or concealed plumbing/drainage | Licensed Plumber boundary; hidden water or sewage damage[^pub-plumbing] | Record leak pattern; ask for scope and testing |
| Unsafe HDB window or exterior air-con unit | Falling-object risk and approved/trained worker requirements[^hdb-windows][^hdb-aircon] | Keep people clear; call approved contractor/installer |
| Suspected asbestos-containing material | Survey and approved removal requirement if present[^mom-asbestos] | Leave material undisturbed; arrange competent identification |
| Sealed refrigerant system or major ventilation changes | Specialist work, equipment and safety/installation requirements[^hdb-aircon] | Report symptoms; compare qualified service proposals |
| Unknown serious hazard: heat, gas smell, spreading dampness, loose concrete | Consequences can develop before the cause is visible | Stop cosmetic work, secure the area, call the relevant professional or emergency service |

**Permit rule:** A permit requirement triggers HDB approval and the correct execution route; it does **not** automatically make every task red. Conversely, an HDB “no permit” item may still require a licensed worker or have technical conditions.[^hdb-permit]
