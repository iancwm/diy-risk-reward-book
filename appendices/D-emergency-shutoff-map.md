# Appendix D: Emergency Shutoff and Contact Map

Complete this page while the home is calm. Walk the flat, identify the controls from labels and official guidance, and ask a qualified person to confirm anything uncertain. Do not dismantle or test an unfamiliar electrical or gas installation to fill in a form.

## Flat sketch

Draw a simple plan showing entrance, kitchen, bathrooms, water sub-meter, consumer unit, gas equipment if present, and the safest exit. Mark controls with the codes below.

```text
                         NORTH / CORRIDOR: __________

  X = exit             F = fire extinguisher (if present)
```

HDB advises closing the stopcock at the PUB water sub-meter when there is a leak.[^hdb-pipes] The consumer unit contains the flat's electrical distribution controls, but electrical repair and replacement belong with a Licensed Electrical Worker.[^hdb-electrical]
