# Appendix C: HDB Permit and Licensed-Worker Check

Use this checklist before an HDB flat project. A **No** under “HDB permit” does not answer the licensed-worker question. Check the current official page for the precise task and flat; keep a screenshot or link with the project record.[^hdb-permit]

## 1. Define the task

- [ ] I have described the exact component and location, including what will be opened, removed, fixed, or connected.
- [ ] I have checked whether the work touches a household shelter, external wall, window, common area, floor slab, bathroom finish, concealed service, or another flat.
- [ ] I have identified the relevant HDB guideline page and its date of access: ____________________.

## 2. HDB approval and conditions

- [ ] The exact work is listed as **permit required**. Application/permit reference: ____________________.
- [ ] The exact work is listed as **no HDB permit required**, and I have recorded its technical conditions: ____________________.
- [ ] The work is not listed, differs from a listed detail, or I cannot classify it; I will ask HDB before starting. Query/reference: ____________________.
- [ ] I have checked any special flat or precinct conditions and timing/noise restrictions relevant to the work.

HDB says work that is absent from its guidance or differs from the stated technical details should be referred to HDB before it starts. Work without a permit must still comply with the applicable conditions.[^hdb-permit]

## 3. Who may execute it?

- [ ] Electrical installation work: I have checked EMA requirements and recorded the Licensed Electrical Worker and licence verification: ____________________.[^ema-electrical]
- [ ] Water-service or sanitary plumbing: I have checked PUB's regulated-versus-simple-work list for this exact task. If regulated, Licensed Plumber details: ____________________.[^pub-plumbing]
- [ ] Gas service work: I have identified the licensed gas service worker or authorised provider; no homeowner gas work is planned.[^hdb-plumbing]
- [ ] Window, air-conditioner, structural, or other specialist work: I have checked HDB's named contractor or qualified-person requirement. Details: ____________________.[^hdb-permit]
- [ ] Renovation contractor: I have checked whether HDB requires a contractor listed in its Directory of Renovation Contractors for this scope. Details: ____________________.[^hdb-permit]

## 4. Completion record

- [ ] Written scope and quotation saved.
- [ ] Approval/permit and licensed-worker details saved where applicable.
- [ ] Before-and-after photos and any test/inspection record saved.
- [ ] Changes and defects recorded; unresolved items assigned to a person and date.

**Decision before work:** ☐ Proceed within documented conditions  ☐ Commission licensed/qualified execution  ☐ Ask HDB or regulator  ☐ Do not proceed
