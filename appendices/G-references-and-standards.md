# Appendix G: References and standards

**Scope and access date:** Singapore, with HDB flats as the principal setting. Links below were checked on 28 September 2026 for this working draft. Recheck the live page, its effective date, and the exact project scope before publication or work. The linked authority pages explain requirements; a named Singapore Standard may have a separate, controlled full text. This index is a reader reference and a starting point for the publication bibliography.

## HDB renovation and flat care

- **S03 — HDB, [Application for a Renovation Permit](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/application-for-a-renovation-permit).** Permit application and the distinction between approval and execution.
- **S07 — HDB, [Important Information on Renovations](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/important-information-on-renovations).** General renovation conditions, contractor requirements, hours, and waste responsibilities.
- **S05 — HDB, [Renovation Guidelines for Building Works](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/renovation-guidelines/building-works).** Building and finish restrictions, including walls and bathrooms.
- **S04 — HDB, [Renovation Guidelines for Electrical Works](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/renovation-guidelines/electrical-works).** HDB-specific electrical renovation conditions.
- **S06 — HDB, [Renovation Guidelines for Water and Sanitary Plumbing Works and Gas Works](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/renovation-guidelines/water-and-sanitary-plumbing-works-and-gas-works).** HDB plumbing and gas conditions.
- **S18 — HDB, [Looking for Renovation Contractors](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/looking-for-renovation-contractors).** Directory of Renovation Contractors and the limits of directory listing.
- **S08 — HDB, [Fixing Clogged or Leaking Pipes in HDB Flats](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/home-maintenance/home-care-guide/pipes).** Water shutoff and pipe maintenance.
- **S10 — HDB, [Maintaining Electrical Accessories and Wiring in HDB Flats](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/sitecore/content/hdbinfoweb/home/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/home-maintenance/home-care-guide/electrical-accessories-and-wiring).** Electrical accessories and qualified-worker boundary.
- **S11 — HDB, [Keeping Windows of HDB Flats in Good Condition](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/home-maintenance/home-care-guide/windows).** Window maintenance and approved contractors.
- **S12 — HDB, [Fixing Choked Floor Traps in HDB Flats](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/home-maintenance/home-care-guide/floor-traps).** Accessible cleaning and escalation.
- **S13 — HDB, [Fixing Wall Cracks in HDB Flats](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/home-maintenance/home-care-guide/walls).** Wall-crack observations and response.
- **S14 — HDB, [Maintaining Air Conditioners in HDB Flats](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/home-maintenance/home-care-guide/air-conditioners).** User maintenance and exterior-unit boundary.
- **S15 — HDB, [Preventing and Fixing Ceiling Leaks in HDB Flats](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/home-maintenance/home-care-guide/ceiling-leaks).** Cross-flat leak investigation and responsibilities.

## Licensed work, safety, and disposal

- **S01 — Energy Market Authority (EMA), [Engaging Licensed Electrical Workers](https://www.ema.gov.sg/consumer-information/electricity/engaging-licensed-workers).** Find and engage an LEW; check licence scope.
- **S02 — PUB, [Plumbing Works](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Professionals/Requirements/Licensed-Plumbers/Plumbing-Works).** Regulated and listed simple plumbing works; confirm the exact job.
- **S09 — EMA, [Gas Safety](https://www.ema.gov.sg/consumer-information/gas/gas-safety).** Current response to suspected piped-gas leaks.
- **S16 — EMA, [Engaging Licensed Gas Service Workers](https://www.ema.gov.sg/consumer-information/gas/engaging-licensed-workers).** Gas installation and handover.
- **S17 — Ministry of Manpower, [Suspected asbestos materials in a house or workplace](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/notify-for-asbestos-work/if-i-suspect-that-i-have-asbestos-materials-in-my-house-or-workplace-what-should-i-do).** Survey and removal route.
- **S19 — National Environment Agency (NEA), [Where to Recycle E-waste](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/waste-management/3r-programmes-and-resources/e-waste-management/where-to-recycle-e-waste).** Collection channels for covered electrical and electronic items.
- **S20 — NEA, [Bulk Item Disposal](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/waste-management/waste-disposal/bulk-item-disposal-disposal-of-garden-wastes-and-dead-pets).** Bulky items and Town Council arrangements.

## Standards named in this manuscript

- **S21 — SS 638, Code of Practice for Electrical Installations.** Listed on [EMA's Singapore Standards and Technical References page](https://www.ema.gov.sg/regulations-licences/regulations/standards-guidelines/singapore-standards-and-technical-references). The book uses the listing to identify the standard, not to instruct a homeowner to design or alter an electrical installation.
- **S22 — SS 636:2018, Code of Practice on Water Services.** Identified on [PUB's Code of Practices page](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Professionals/Resources/Code-of-Practices?itemsperpage=20). Check the current edition and PUB requirements for professional work.
- **S23 — SS 578 and SS EN 3, portable fire extinguisher standards.** Referenced by [Singapore Civil Defence Force's Portable Fire Extinguisher page](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/fire-safety-services-listing/permits-and-certifications/portable-fire-extinguisher). Their appearance here is for product and compliance context, not a substitute for fire-safety training.
