# 12. Economics of DIY

DIY has a price even when no one sends an invoice. Materials, tools, travel, preparation, cleanup, and rework all use resources. A professional quote also has a scope: one price may include protection, testing, disposal, and a return visit while another does not. Compare the same finished result, not two headline figures.

## Price the whole job

Write a shopping list for the *smallest useful scope*. The material cost is the quantity actually purchased, including whole tins, packs, spare parts, and delivery—not just the amount consumed. Add consumables and a fair share of reusable tools. Decide how to value your time; this is a personal opportunity-cost choice, not a Singapore wage claim. Include permits, licensed work, or required testing in whichever option needs them.

| Cost component | DIY entry | Professional quote entry |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Materials and delivery | S$___ | Included / S$___ |
| Consumables, protection, disposal | S$___ | Included / S$___ |
| New tool cost or allocated share | S$___ | Usually included |
| Homeowner time (___ hours × chosen value) | S$___ | Access and supervision time: S$___ |
| Permit, inspection, or testing | S$___ | Included / S$___ |
| Plausible rework allowance | S$___ | Contract and warranty terms: ___ |
| **Comparable total** | **S$___** | **S$___** |

The last row is not a decision by itself. Ask whether an ordinary failure is visible and cheap to correct. If a leak could be hidden in a cabinet or a fix might affect another flat, do not convert that risk into a casual “10% contingency.” The outcome may be too uncertain for a useful probability estimate; use the stop rule and qualified execution instead.

## Dated retail examples, not project quotes

The examples below were visible on Singapore retailer pages on **28 September 2026**. They show how different the *product* cost can be from a complete job. They are not recommendations, installation quotes, or guaranteed future prices. Check compatibility, quantity, delivery, and current stock before using any figure.

| Example item | Listed price | What the price leaves out |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 5 L interior wall paint | S$85[^paint] | Primer, filler, roller, trays, coverings, and any unused paint |
| 260 mL sanitary silicone | S$6.50[^silicone] | Removal, preparation, caulk gun, cure time, and any leak diagnosis |
| Two cabinet handles | S$9.90[^handles] | Hole compatibility and installation time |
| One wall shelf | S$19.90[^shelf] | Wall screws, suitable anchors, wall assessment, installation |
| One basic kitchen mixer tap | S$59[^tap] | Hoses or other compatible parts, leak checking, and any plumber work |

For example, four handles from S$9.90 two-packs would require two packs, or S$19.80 before travel and time. That is a useful green-project calculation because the likely correction is small. A S$59 tap has a different risk profile: if the shutoff is corroded or a connection is hidden, the product price says little about the safe installed cost. Do not carry a green project's savings logic into a water or electrical job.

## Decide whether to buy the tool

For a reusable tool, divide its purchase price by the number of *realistic* future uses, then add storage, maintenance, and learning time. If a drill costs S$___ and you have four confirmed appropriate jobs, the assigned cost per job is one quarter of that price; it is not zero. Borrowing or renting can be sensible for an infrequent tool when the task itself is within your skill and authority. Buying a multimeter does not reduce the professional requirement for electrical installation work in an HDB flat.[^ema-electrical]

## The economics of hiring well

Ask for a written scope, not just a total. What does the price include: licensed execution, HDB submission, protection, product, testing, disposal, defects, and return visits? HDB notes that engaging a renovation contractor is a private contract and that a DRC listing is not its guarantee of work quality.[^hdb-contractors] Compare quotes line by line and keep records of changes. A higher complete price may be cheaper than a low initial price followed by variations or an untested repair.

There is also a value to learning and enjoyment. It can justify a green project even when the dollar saving is modest. For red work, the best investment is often the time spent writing the brief and checking the professional's scope.
