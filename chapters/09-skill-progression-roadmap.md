# 9. Skill Progression Roadmap

Build skill by adding one kind of uncertainty at a time. A completed course, a new tool, or a successful project does not remove a licensing requirement. Progress means better judgement about scope and better work within the scope you are allowed to do.

## Level 0 — Know the Flat

Walk through the home and fill in the shutoff map in Appendix D. Find the water stopcock and consumer unit without opening electrical equipment. Read the relevant HDB home-care pages. Learn to distinguish a cosmetic surface defect from a leak, heat, movement, or recurring failure. Practise measuring and documenting a project before buying materials.

## Level 1 — Reversible Finishes and Assembly

Start with painting a sound dry wall, replacing cabinet handles, and assembling freestanding furniture. Follow product instructions and keep the first job small enough to correct. Photograph the result after use, not just immediately after completion. Learn the habits of protecting surfaces, cleaning up, and disposing of waste properly.

## Level 2 — Bounded Fixings and Simple Maintenance

Move to light-duty mounting or a listed simple plumbing task only after checking the exact HDB wall or plumbing conditions. Learn to identify a safe support, use the specified fastener, and stop at uncertain material or a concealed service. PUB's list of simple plumbing works provides a useful starting point for task-by-task screening; it is not permission to alter pipes generally.[^pub-plumbing]

## Level 3 — Yellow Tasks With Preparation

Take a class, watch a qualified person, or practise on a non-installed mock-up where that helps. Then choose a yellow task only if it has a legal homeowner scope, a written stop rule, safe isolation, and a reliable result check. The outcome may be a supervised task or a professional installation that you planned well. In Singapore, replacing electrical installation components is not a DIY graduation exercise; EMA directs consumers to use Licensed Electrical Workers.[^ema-electrical]

## Level 4 — Become an Informed Client

For red work, learn to describe symptoms, ask about licences and approvals, compare scope and testing, and retain records. This is a higher level of home stewardship, not a failure to progress. A homeowner who notices a concealed leak early, commissions the right specialist, and checks the completion documents has used the framework well.

At every level, keep a short project log: what you attempted, what you learned, what you would change, and what you will not attempt again. Skills transfer; permissions and site conditions do not. Recheck the rule for each new job.

[^pub-plumbing]: PUB, [Plumbing Works](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Professionals/Requirements/Licensed-Plumbers/Plumbing-Works), accessed 28 September 2026.
[^ema-electrical]: Energy Market Authority, [Engaging Licensed Electrical Workers](https://www.ema.gov.sg/consumer-information/electricity/engaging-licensed-workers), accessed 28 September 2026.
